    About

    RUBRIC classifies the base pairs of an RNA structure in the Leontis-Westhof scheme and scores each pair from 0 to 100 against pairs of the same type and class in high-quality structures. It also assigns each backbone suite to a conformer, suggests target torsions for suites that fit poorly, and flags riboses whose pucker is inconsistent.

    Every Thursday, the day after the weekly PDB release, this site scores the RNA entries that are new or were revised and removes entries that were made obsolete. All results come from one RUBRIC version, shown at the top of the page.

    Pair tiers

    • Preferred score 100
    • Secondary review score 80 or more, below 100
    • Priority review score below 80

    Ambiguous pairs, whose geometry lies between two classes, and pairs involving DNA are not scored.

    Use

    The results are free to use under CC BY 4.0. Any structure, including unpublished models, can be checked locally with pip install rubric-rna.
